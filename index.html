Emergency Plumbing Services in San Antonio

Every service below is available right now, not just during business hours. Our emergency plumbers carry the parts for the most common San Antonio failures, so most repairs are finished in one visit, not scheduled for later.

Burst or Leaking Pipe Repair

A pipe that suddenly starts spraying or leaking can cause serious water damage in a short time. You may notice water coming through a ceiling, a wet spot on the wall, pooling under a sink, or a sudden drop in water pressure. If you can safely reach your home's main water shutoff, turn the water off first. Then call an emergency plumber before the leak causes more damage to drywall, flooring, cabinets, or insulation. We locate the damaged section of pipe and determine whether it can be repaired or needs to be replaced. Depending on the plumbing system, we can repair or replace sections of copper, PEX, galvanized, or other residential water lines.

Slab Leak Detection and Repair

A slab leak is a water leak in a pipe beneath or inside the concrete foundation of your home. Common warning signs include unexplained water usage, the sound of running water when everything is turned off, warm spots on the floor, damp flooring, low water pressure, or water appearing around the foundation. Because the pipe is below concrete, finding the leak accurately is important before breaking into the slab. We use leak detection and pressure testing to narrow down the location and determine what is leaking. Once the source is identified, we explain the available repair options. Depending on the location and condition of the pipe, that may include accessing the damaged section directly or rerouting the affected line.

Water Heater Repair and Replacement

A water heater problem can mean anything from no hot water to an active leak that is damaging the area around the tank. If you have water around the base of a tank, unusual noises, inconsistent hot water, or a unit that repeatedly shuts off, the heater should be inspected. For an electric or gas water heater, turn the unit off if you know how to do so safely and shut off the water supply if the tank is actively leaking. Do not attempt to repair a gas connection yourself. We diagnose the cause of the problem and repair components when practical. If the water heater is beyond economical repair, we can help you choose a properly sized replacement and handle the installation.

Emergency Drain Cleaning

One slow drain is usually different from several drains backing up at the same time. If water comes up through a shower when the toilet is flushed, or multiple sinks, tubs, and toilets stop draining, the blockage may be in the home's main sewer line. Stop using water throughout the house until the blockage is cleared. Continuing to run sinks, showers, washing machines, or toilets can force more wastewater into the home. Our emergency drain service can clear serious blockages using professional drain-cleaning equipment. When the cause is unclear or the problem keeps returning, a sewer camera inspection can help identify issues such as roots, grease buildup, damaged pipe, or heavy mineral deposits.

Sewer Backup and Sewer Line Problems

A sewer backup is more than an inconvenient clogged drain. Wastewater coming into a bathtub, toilet, floor drain, or other fixture can expose your family to harmful contaminants and can quickly damage flooring and other materials. Avoid contact with the wastewater and stop using your plumbing until the source is addressed. If the backup is affecting multiple fixtures, the problem may be in the main sewer line rather than a single drain. We first work to restore drainage and then determine why the line backed up. A sewer camera can reveal root intrusion, cracked or separated pipe, collapsed sections, or other problems inside the line. From there, we can explain whether a localized repair, pipe replacement, or another solution is appropriate.

Gas Line Leak

If you smell natural gas inside or around your home, treat it as an emergency. Do not try to find the leak yourself, operate electrical switches, or create a flame to locate the source. Leave the building and contact the appropriate gas emergency service from a safe location. In San Antonio, CPS Energy handles gas emergencies and can secure the gas supply. Once the immediate gas emergency has been addressed, a licensed plumber can inspect and repair the plumbing system as needed. Gas line repairs require proper testing and must comply with applicable safety and plumbing requirements. We can locate the source of a plumbing-side gas leak, make the necessary repair, and test the system before it is returned to service.

Emergency Toilet and Fixture Problems

An overflowing toilet can put dirty water onto your bathroom floor within minutes. If the toilet will not stop running or flushing, turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise to stop the water supply. If the toilet continues to overflow after the valve is closed, or if other fixtures are backing up too, the problem may be more serious than a simple toilet clog. We can clear toilet blockages, replace failed fill valves and flappers, repair leaking supply lines, and diagnose problems that are connected to the home's larger drainage system. Before completing the job, we also check for signs that the plumbing failure has caused water damage around the fixture.

Main Water Line and Shutoff Valve Problems

A leak in the main water line can waste a large amount of water even when every faucet in your home is turned off. Signs can include a constantly moving water meter, unexplained water usage, wet areas in the yard, unusually low pressure, or water collecting near the foundation. If you suspect a main line leak, shut off your home's main water valve if it is accessible and working. This can help limit water loss while you wait for an emergency plumber. We locate the leak and determine whether the problem is on the homeowner's side of the water meter or part of the public water system. For homeowner-side leaks, we repair or replace the damaged section and can also address faulty or inaccessible shutoff valves.

Commercial Emergency Plumbing

A plumbing emergency can interrupt business operations just as quickly as it can disrupt a home. A blocked restroom, overflowing drain, broken water line, or plumbing leak can force a restaurant, office, apartment property, or retail business to close part of the building. We provide emergency plumbing service for San Antonio businesses when a problem cannot wait until the next business day. Depending on the situation, this can include drain and sewer problems, leaking pipes, water heaters, fixtures, backflow-related plumbing issues, and other urgent repairs. The goal is to stop the immediate problem, protect the property, and get the plumbing system operating normally again as quickly as practical.

San Antonio Plumbing Emergencies Are Different

An emergency plumber San Antonio homeowners can rely on has to understand three things about this city. First, our water. San Antonio pulls from the Edwards Aquifer, and hardness runs 15 to 20 grains per gallon. That scale chews through water heater elements, fill valves, and cartridge stems years earlier than in soft water cities.

Second, our soil. The expansive clay under most of Bexar County moves with every wet and dry cycle, and it is the main reason slab leaks are so common here. Third, our housing. Thousands of homes built before 1980 still run original cast iron drains and copper under the slab. We know what is inside the walls of a 1960s Jefferson bungalow and a 2000s Stone Oak two story, because we open both every week.

That local knowledge changes the repair. A generic fix fails here. The right one accounts for hard water, clay soil, and City of San Antonio code, including permits for water heater and sewer work.

What counts as a plumbing emergency versus something that can wait?

If water or sewage is actively damaging your home, it is an emergency. If you can stop it with a valve, it can usually wait for a scheduled visit.

Call now for burst pipes, sewage backing up into tubs or floor drains, any gas smell, no water to the whole house, or a toilet overflowing that will not stop. A slow drip from one fixture, a running toilet, or one slow drain can wait until morning once you close the angle stop under the fixture. Not sure? Call anyway. What should I do before the emergency plumber arrives?

Shut off your main water valve first. That single step prevents more damage than anything else we do later.

  1. Find the main shut off. It is usually in the garage, a front yard valve box, or on an outside wall. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Switch off the water heater at the breaker or gas valve so it does not fire dry.
  3. Move electronics and furniture away from the water, and put down towels.
  4. Clear the path to the water heater, cleanout, or problem fixture.
I smell gas near my water heater. What do I do right now?

Leave the house immediately, do not touch any light switches or outlets, and call CPS Energy at 210-353-4357 from outside. That is their 24 hour emergency line, and they will shut the meter and secure the line.

Once the utility clears the site, call us. We pressure test the gas system, locate the leak, repair it to code, and coordinate the City of San Antonio inspection so your gas can be restored. Never test a gas smell with a flame, and do not re enter until the utility says it is safe.

Why do San Antonio homes get slab leaks so often?

Three local factors stack up: expansive clay soil, very hard water, and aging underground lines.

The clay soil under most of Bexar County swells when it rains and shrinks during drought, flexing the pipes under your slab until they crack. Edwards Aquifer water runs 15 to 20 grains per gallon of hardness, which scales and thins copper from the inside. And thousands of local homes are 40 plus years old with original pipe. Warm spots on the floor, a meter spinning with everything off, or a sudden jump in your SAWS bill are the classic early signs. Caught early, a reroute is usually far cheaper than the water damage.

Will homeowners insurance cover my plumbing emergency?

Usually yes for sudden and accidental damage like a burst pipe. Usually no for slow leaks that went unaddressed.

Texas policies typically cover the resulting water damage, and many cover the cost of tearing out and replacing whatever it takes to reach the failed pipe. The pipe repair itself varies by policy. We photograph everything, document the cause in writing, and give you an itemized invoice formatted the way adjusters expect. The final call belongs to your insurer, but we make their job easy.

Sewage backing up into the house, an active leak you cannot contain, a gas odor, and a water heater dumping from the tank seam. Those four get a truck tonight, every time.
