What Makes a Drain Problem an Emergency?

Not every slow drain needs an emergency call. But when water is backing up into your home or multiple fixtures stop draining at once, the problem is in your main line and will not resolve on its own.

Call Immediately If You See

  • Raw sewage backing up into sinks, tubs, or floor drains
  • Toilet overflowing repeatedly and a plunger does not help
  • Water backing up in one fixture when you use a different one

  • Foul sewer odor entering your home through drains or vents

Grease, food particles, and soap scum build up in kitchen lines over time. Hair and soap scum are the usual culprits in bathroom drains. We clear clogged shower drains, bathtub drains, and sink drains completely, not just the visible surface buildup. If the same drain keeps clogging, we check for deeper issues in the branch line.

Sewer Backup Cleanup

Sewage backing up into your home is a health hazard that needs immediate attention. We stop the backup, clear the line, and help you understand what caused it so it does not happen again. If the backup has caused water damage, we can recommend next steps for cleanup.

Overflowing Floor Drain

Floor drains in laundry rooms, garages, and basements overflow when the main line is blocked downstream. We clear the blockage and restore proper drainage before water damage spreads. This is often the first sign of a main sewer line problem.

Local Conditions

Why Drains Clog More Often in San Antonio

San Antonio has specific conditions that make drain problems more common and more severe than in many other cities. Knowing what causes clogs helps you prevent them and helps us clear them faster when they happen.

01

Grease Buildup

Cooking oils and fats pour down the kitchen drain as liquids but solidify as they cool inside the pipe. Over time, this layer narrows the pipe until water cannot pass. This is the number one cause of kitchen drain clogs in San Antonio homes.

02

Hair and Soap Scum

Bathroom drains collect hair that combines with soap residue to form a sticky mass. This mass catches more debris and grows until the drain stops completely. Shower and bathtub drains are the most common locations.

03

Tree Root Intrusion

San Antonio's mature oak trees send roots toward the moisture in sewer lines. Roots enter through small cracks or loose joints and grow inside the pipe until they catch enough debris to cause a full blockage. This is a leading cause of main sewer line backups in older neighborhoods like Alamo Heights and Monte Vista.

04

Mineral Scale from Hard Water

San Antonio water comes from the Edwards Aquifer and carries high levels of dissolved minerals. These minerals deposit on the inside of pipes, gradually narrowing them and giving debris more surface to catch on. This is why drain cleaning in San Antonio often requires more than a simple snake.

05

Foreign Objects

Small items flushed or dropped down drains, such as wipes, paper towels, and small toys, can lodge in pipe bends and create an immediate blockage. Unlike gradual buildup, these cause sudden backups. Flushable wipes are a common culprit because they do not break down like toilet paper.

Our Process

How We Clear Your Drain

Every drain clog is different. A kitchen grease clog needs a different approach than tree roots in a main line. Here is how we work.

01 Diagnose

Diagnose the Blockage

We start by determining whether the clog is in a single fixture line or the main sewer line. If multiple fixtures are affected, we know the problem is deeper. For recurring clogs or main line issues, we use camera inspection to see exactly what is happening inside the pipe.

02 Method

Choose the Right Method

A standard sink clog might need only a mechanical auger. Heavy grease or scale buildup calls for hydro jetting. Tree roots require root cutting equipment. We match the method to the blockage and the pipe material, not a one size fits all approach.

03 Clear

Clear the Line Completely

Our goal is a full clear, not a temporary hole through the middle of the clog. We remove the entire obstruction so the drain flows freely and does not back up again in a few weeks. This is the difference between a quick fix and a lasting repair.

04 Verify

Verify and Test

After clearing, we run water through the system to confirm full flow. For main line work, we use camera inspection to verify the pipe is clear and check for any damage the blockage may have caused. If we find damage, we explain your options before leaving.

Snaking vs Hydro Jetting

Drain Snaking vs Hydro Jetting: Which Method Is Right?

Homeowners often ask why we do not just snake every drain. The answer is that different clogs need different tools. Using the wrong method can damage pipes or leave the clog partially in place.

Standard

Drain Snaking (Cabling)

Snaking is the standard method for common clogs caused by hair, paper, or localized blockage near a fixture. A professional drain snake breaks through or pulls apart the clog so water can move again. It is often the best first solution for isolated drain clogs.

However, snaking does not clean the pipe walls. If grease or scale coats the inside of the pipe, snaking may only open a small channel that clogs again quickly.

Heavy Duty

Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting uses high pressure water to clean the inside of the pipe more thoroughly. Instead of only punching through the blockage, it removes grease, sludge, soap residue, mineral buildup, and other debris coating the pipe walls.

It is especially useful for recurring clogs, heavy buildup, and some sewer line cleaning situations. Because it is powerful, hydro jetting should only be done by a professional who has inspected the pipe first.

Diagnostic

When Camera Inspection Is Needed

If multiple drains are slow, wastewater backs up in more than one fixture, or clogs keep returning despite previous cleaning, the problem may be deeper in the sewer line.

Camera inspection lets us look inside the pipe and identify tree roots, pipe corrosion, bellied sections, misaligned joints, cracks, or collapsed pipe. This step matters because it helps you avoid guessing and paying for the wrong fix.

Before We Arrive

What to Do While You Wait for Your Plumber

These steps limit damage and keep your household safe until our plumber arrives.

  1. 1

    Stop Using All Water Fixtures

    Every gallon you send down the drain adds to the backup. Do not run sinks, showers, or washing machines.

  2. 2

    Do Not Flush Toilets

    If the main line is blocked, flushing will cause sewage to back up through floor drains or tubs.

  3. 3

    Shut Off Water to Overflowing Fixtures

    If a toilet or sink is overflowing, close the local shutoff valve if you can reach it safely.

  4. 4

    Move Valuables Away From Affected Areas

    Water and sewage can damage flooring, rugs, and furniture. Create a barrier with towels if the backup is spreading.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does emergency drain cleaning cost in San Antonio?

The cost depends on the type of drain and the severity of the blockage. A single fixture clog cleared with a standard auger typically costs less than a main sewer line service or hydro jetting. We confirm the price before any work begins, so you know the cost upfront. Emergency calls outside normal business hours may have an additional dispatch fee.

Do you really answer calls at 2 AM?

Yes. Drain emergencies do not follow business hours. When you call, you reach a real person who can dispatch a plumber, not an answering service that promises a callback in the morning.

Can I use chemical drain cleaner instead of calling a plumber?

Chemical cleaners can damage pipes, especially older cast iron or PVC lines, and they often do not clear the full blockage. They may open a small channel through the clog, which closes again quickly. Mechanical clearing removes the entire obstruction. If you have already used a chemical cleaner, tell the plumber before they start work.

What is the difference between drain cleaning and drain clearing?

People use these terms interchangeably. Both refer to removing a blockage from a drain line. Drain clearing often describes an urgent situation, while drain cleaning can include routine maintenance. In either case, the goal is the same: restore full flow.

How do I know if it is a main sewer line backup?

If more than one drain in your home is slow or backing up at the same time, the problem is almost certainly in the main line. Another sign is water backing up in a shower or tub when you flush a toilet. A single slow drain is usually a local clog.

How can I prevent my drains from clogging again?

Never pour grease down the kitchen drain. Use a drain screen in showers and tubs to catch hair. Do not flush wipes, paper towels, or feminine products. If you have recurring clogs in the same drain, schedule a camera inspection to find out why.
